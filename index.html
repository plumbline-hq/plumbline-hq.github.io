What this is

More and more purchases, signups, and API calls are made by software agents rather than people. Most systems were never tested against that kind of customer. Plumbline tests them: honestly, reproducibly, and with a receipt attached to every claim.

Research

We publish what we measure, including results that cut against us: the x402 economy is smaller than reported and shrinking, 18% of catalogued paid endpoints fail an agent, and llms.txt is barely read — which made us downgrade our own check.

Operating rules

Every finding is independently reproducible or it doesn't ship. Findings bind to receipts. Errors get corrected publicly with dated amendments, never silent edits. No system is tested without its owner's explicit permission. No client funds are ever custodied.
